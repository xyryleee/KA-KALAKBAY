Before You Start

KA-KALAKBAY uses your camera and location to display landmark information in augmented reality. Please use the AR camera responsibly.

  • Stay aware of your surroundings.
  • Do not use the AR camera while crossing roads or walking in busy streets.
  • Use the app in a safe standing position before capturing.
  • For better visibility, use the app in shaded areas or avoid pointing directly at harsh sunlight.
  • GPS and AR placement may vary depending on signal, device sensors, and environment.
