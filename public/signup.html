KA-KALAKBAY Terms and Conditions

Welcome to KA-KALAKBAY, a web-based augmented reality tourism application designed to help users explore selected landmarks in Olongapo City through maps, landmark information, location-based AR, and achievement features.

Purpose of the Application

KA-KALAKBAY is intended for educational, tourism, and informational purposes. The application provides landmark details, historical descriptions, location-based AR experiences, map features, and achievement tracking. GPS, AR, and map readings may vary depending on device capability, signal strength, environment, and internet connection.

User Responsibility

Users must use KA-KALAKBAY safely and responsibly. Always remain aware of your surroundings, especially when using the AR camera outdoors. Do not use the app while crossing roads or walking in unsafe areas. Respect public spaces, landmarks, private property, and other people.

Data Privacy

KA-KALAKBAY values your privacy. The application only collects and processes data necessary for the app to function properly. This may include your email address, authentication information, landmark visit progress, selected landmark data, approximate device location while using geofence or AR features, and camera access while using the AR feature.

KA-KALAKBAY does not sell, trade, or share personal user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties. User data is used only for account access, saving progress, displaying achievements, enabling AR/map features, and improving the user experience.

Location Data

Location access is used to calculate your distance from selected landmarks, check whether you are within the required geofence, enable location-based AR content, and support map-related features. Your location is used only when the app is active and only when you grant permission through your browser or device.

Camera Access

Camera access is required for the AR experience. KA-KALAKBAY does not secretly record or monitor users through the camera. The camera is only activated when the user opens the AR feature and grants permission. If the app includes a capture feature, images are only captured when the user intentionally presses the capture button.

Third-Party Services

KA-KALAKBAY may use Firebase for authentication, database storage, and hosting. It may also use libraries and services such as Leaflet, OpenStreetMap, A-Frame, and AR.js for maps and augmented reality features.

Limitation of Liability

KA-KALAKBAY is a project-based application and may contain technical limitations, GPS inaccuracies, AR placement errors, map inaccuracies, or compatibility issues. The developers are not liable for accidents, injuries, damages, or incidents caused by unsafe or distracted use of the application.
